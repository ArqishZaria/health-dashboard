Tips
  • Always start from the "Download Template" button above for your chosen form type - the headers must match exactly for a row to import correctly. The template's "Instructions" sheet has the full details.
  • Dates can be entered in several common formats (DD-MM-YYYY, DD/MM/YYYY, "05 March 2026", etc.). If a sheet has an adjacent "Month" column, it's used to resolve ambiguous dates (e.g. 03/04/2026); with no Month column, day-first is assumed.
  • Not sure how a Region, Local Council or Jamatkhana is spelled in the system? Download the Region / Local Council / Jamatkhana Reference.
  • Region, Local Council and Jamatkhana values are cross-checked against existing records only, and are also checked against your own assigned scope.
  • Unrecognised columns are preserved and stored against each record - nothing is silently dropped.
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB.
